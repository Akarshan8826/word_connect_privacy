Word Connect

A word puzzle game by Akarshan

Word Connect (“the App”) is developed and maintained by Akarshan. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information to the developer or to any third party.

The App stores the following information locally on your device only, using Android’s standard local storage:

  • Your in-app coin balance
  • Your level progress and star ratings
  • Your gameplay settings

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer.

2. Third-Party Services

This version of the App does not integrate advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or third-party data-collection services. If this changes in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

3. Children’s Privacy

The App is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

4. Data Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal data, it does not hold personal data on the developer’s servers. Game progress stored locally is sandboxed by Android and is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

5. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page and, where appropriate, in the app listing.
